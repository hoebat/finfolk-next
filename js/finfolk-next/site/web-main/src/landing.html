Features

01 / LOCATIONS

Pick your spot.

Add the spots you dive to your profile. Find the Regulars and freedivers who visit the same places. You choose who can see your profile.

02 / LOGS

Post your logs.

Record your freediving and training sessions. Keep them private or publish them.

03 / COMMUNITY

Discover your FinFolk.

Check in at your locations, meet the Regulars and connect with other freedivers.

INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK

Use @finfolk.com.au.

Connect your accounts and tag your photos and videos. FinFolk brings supported posts into your private log with their day and matched location, ready to review and sort into a session.

04 / INSTAGRAM COMMANDS

Connect. Then check in.

Follow @finfolk.com.au and send /connect on Instagram. Enter the code in FinFolk, then message /checkin Brighton Beach to record a visit.

Commands are available when Instagram messaging is enabled. A check-in records a visit, not a dive log.

05 / INSTAGRAM POSTS & REELS

Tag your session.

Tag @finfolk.com.au in your Instagram photo, post or Reel. It arrives in your FinFolk log with the post’s day and matched location, ready to sort into a session.

For connected Instagram accounts with tag imports enabled. Check the day and location, add more information and choose when to publish your session.

06 / TIKTOK VIDEOS

Bring your video.

Connect TikTok and mention @finfolk.com.au in your public video. FinFolk imports it with its post day and matched location so you can add details and sort it into a session.

Automatic imports require TikTok account authorization and enabled provider access. Imports stay private until you choose to publish the session.

07 / REGULARS

Become a Regular.

Check in on three separate days within the last 30 days to become a Regular at a location. Find other freedivers and view their shared Instagram and TikTok profiles.

  1. 01Check in
  2. 02Keep coming back
  3. 033rd day: become a Regular

Visits are self-reported. You choose your profile and what you share with other divers.
